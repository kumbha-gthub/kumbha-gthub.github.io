Efficient Conditioning: Why Pseudo-Observation Batch Bayesian Optimization Works (When It Doesn't) Under Review (NeurIPS 2026)
Kumbha Nagaswetha
Preprint, 2026

This work introduces the concept of efficient conditioning, a theoretical property explaining why pseudo-observation methods such as Constant Liar, Kriging Believer, and fantasy models generate diverse batch recommendations when using Gaussian Process surrogates, while parametric surrogate models often collapse to nearly identical solutions. The paper establishes theoretical guarantees and validates the analysis on Hartmann-6D, Ackley-8D, Levy-10D, and SVM hyperparameter optimization benchmarks.

HDR Image Reconstruction using Unsupervised Learning
Kumbha Nagaswetha
arXiv Preprint, 2025

Developed an unsupervised deep learning framework for reconstructing high dynamic range (HDR) images from multi-exposure inputs without requiring ground-truth HDR supervision. The proposed learning objective combines structural similarity and perceptual quality measures, producing high-quality HDR reconstructions while remaining completely unsupervised.
