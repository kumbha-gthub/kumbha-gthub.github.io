Research & Industrial Projects

My work spans scientific machine learning, computer vision, machine health monitoring, and optimization for engineering systems. Below is a selection of representative research and industrial projects.

Research Projects

U-FNO: Fourier Neural Operators for Robust Domain Adaptation
2025–2026

Developed U-FNO, a Fourier Neural Operator-based architecture for robust domain adaptation in bearing fault diagnosis under sensor and operating-condition shifts. The model combines spectral learning, multi-scale feature extraction, and CORAL-based feature alignment to improve cross-domain generalization while requiring only limited target-domain labels.

Efficient Batch Bayesian Optimization
2025–Present

Developed theoretical foundations explaining why pseudo-observation methods such as Constant Liar and Kriging Believer succeed with Gaussian Processes but fail for many parametric surrogate models. Introduced the notion of efficient conditioning and established provable guarantees for diverse batch generation.

HDR Image Reconstruction using Unsupervised Learning
2021–2025

Developed an unsupervised deep-learning framework that reconstructs HDR images from multi-exposure inputs without requiring ground-truth HDR supervision. Designed a novel learning objective combining structural similarity and perceptual quality metrics to improve reconstruction quality.

Industrial Projects

Machine Health Monitoring using Domain Adaptation
Atlas Copco · 2024–Present

Developing robust machine learning methods for fault diagnosis under changing operating conditions. The project focuses on cross-domain learning, improved generalization, and data-efficient adaptation for industrial diagnostic systems.

AI-based Aerodynamic Design Optimization
Atlas Copco · 2024–2025

Developing surrogate modeling and scientific machine learning methods to accelerate computational fluid dynamics based engineering design. The work integrates Bayesian optimization with machine learning to reduce computational cost while improving design quality.

Speed Estimation of Compressors from Vibration Signals
Atlas Copco · 2022–2023

Developed machine learning models for estimating compressor operating speed directly from vibration signals using advanced signal processing and deep learning methods.

Lubricant Level Estimation using Audio Signals
Atlas Copco · 2023

Developed CNN-based models for estimating lubricant levels using acoustic measurements for non-invasive industrial monitoring.

Academic Projects

Facial Emotion Recognition
IISc · 2021

Developed facial emotion recognition models using AlexNet feature extraction and Support Vector Machines on JAFFE, AFED, and FER datasets while studying transfer learning and cross-dataset generalization.

Image Classification & Semantic Segmentation
IISc

Implemented image classification using CNN features from AlexNet and ResNet and compared Normalized Cuts with Fully Convolutional Networks for semantic image segmentation.

Image Restoration & Compression
IISc

Worked on BM3D denoising, wavelet denoising, JPEG compression, perceptual quality metrics including SSIM and LPIPS, and image enhancement methods.

Scale-Invariant Feature Transform (SIFT)
IISc

Implemented the complete SIFT pipeline and analyzed robustness to scale, rotation, blur, and noise.

Fuzzy Directional Weighted Median Filter
JNTU Kakinada · 2020

Designed a fuzzy logic-based impulse noise removal algorithm that preserved image edges while outperforming conventional median filtering approaches.
