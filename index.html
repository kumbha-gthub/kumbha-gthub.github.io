About

I am a Senior Data Scientist at Atlas Copco Group working at the intersection of signal processing, scientific machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision. My work focuses on building machine learning systems that remain reliable under distribution shifts while translating efficiently from research into real-world industrial deployment.

I obtained my M.Tech in Signal Processing from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, and my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from JNTU Kakinada. Since 2022, I have been developing machine learning solutions for machine health monitoring, surrogate modeling, scientific machine learning, Bayesian optimization, and industrial AI applications.

My research spans deep learning architectures including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Vision Transformers, Graph Neural Networks, Neural Operators, Physics-Informed Neural Networks, and modern optimization methods for engineering systems. I am particularly interested in combining physical knowledge with data-driven learning to improve robustness, generalization, and interpretability.

Research Overview

My current research focuses on developing reliable machine learning methods for engineering and scientific applications, with emphasis on:

  • Scientific Machine Learning Neural Operators, Physics-Informed Learning, surrogate modeling, and scientific AI.
  • Bayesian Optimization Efficient batch Bayesian optimization, Gaussian processes, and expensive black-box optimization.
  • Computer Vision Robust perception, representation learning, domain adaptation, and learning under distribution shifts.
  • Industrial AI Machine health monitoring, vibration analysis, scientific machine learning, and predictive maintenance.
